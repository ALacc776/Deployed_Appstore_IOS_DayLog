# Privacy Policy for DayLog
*Last updated: October 2026*

> **Quick Summary:** DayLog stores your journal entries locally on your device. If you turn on iCloud Backup, a copy is also saved to your own iCloud Drive. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers. Your privacy is our priority.

## 1. Information We Collect
DayLog is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:
* **Journal Entries:** Your journal entries are stored locally on your device, and in your own iCloud Drive only if you turn on iCloud Backup
* **Usage Data:** Basic app usage statistics (streak counts, entry dates) stored locally
* **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal identifiers

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Your journal entries and data are used solely for:
* Displaying your entries within the app
* Calculating your journaling streak
* Providing app functionality (search, filtering, calendar view)
* Backup and restore features, including optional iCloud Backup

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Your data security is our top priority:
* **Local Storage:** All data is stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage
* **Encryption:** Journal entries are encrypted using iOS Keychain services
* **No Developer Servers:** We do not send your data to our servers. The only outside service DayLog uses is Apple's iCloud Drive, and only when you turn on iCloud Backup
* **Optional iCloud Backup:** If you turn on "Back Up to iCloud Drive", DayLog saves a copy of your journal (a PDF, a backup file, daily snapshots from the last 60 days, and your photos) to the DayLog folder in your own iCloud Drive. These files are not encrypted by DayLog, so you can open them in the Files app and restore them on a new device. They are protected by your Apple Account, and the developer cannot access them. iCloud Backup is off by default.
* **No Third-Party Access:** Your data is not shared with any third parties

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not share your personal information with anyone:
* No data is transmitted to our servers
* No data is shared with third-party services (iCloud Backup stores data in your own Apple account, not with us)
* No analytics or tracking services are used
* Your journal entries remain private to you

## 5. Your Rights
You have complete control over your data:
* **Access:** View all your entries within the app
* **Export:** Export your entries as PDF, Word, JSON, text, or CSV files
* **Delete:** Delete individual entries or all data
* **Backup:** Keep an automatic backup in your iCloud Drive and restore it on any device

## 6. Data Retention
Your data is retained as long as you choose:
* Data remains on your device until you delete it. Copies in your iCloud Drive remain until you delete the DayLog folder in the Files app
* No automatic deletion of your entries
* You can delete all data at any time through app settings

## 7. Children's Privacy
DayLog is appropriate for all ages:
* No personal information is collected from anyone
* Safe for children to use with parental supervision
* No social features or external communication

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be:
* Notified through app updates
* Posted with updated "Last updated" date
* Maintained with the same privacy-first approach
